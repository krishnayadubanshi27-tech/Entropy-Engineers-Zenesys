# Entropy-Engineers-Zenesys

PROJECT:Smart Contract and compliance monitor.
<br><br>
DESCRIPTION:
**EnContract** is an intelligent Web3 and legal-tech platform built to help businesses seamlessly track contracts, agreements, and regulatory requirements. Powered by local AI analysis, interactive visualization, and automated workflows, EnContract eliminates missed obligations, overdue renewals, and compliance blind spots.
<br><br>
## 🎯 Problem Statement

Businesses struggle to track complex agreements, payment milestones, and regulatory compliance deadlines across their operations. Manual tracking leads to missed renewals, unfulfilled obligations, and legal risks. 
<br><br>

**<u>🟢The Solution</u>**
<br>
***EnContract*** solves this by providing an automated workspace that extracts contract metadata, analyzes agreements tied to various services or vendors, visualizes risk  factors, provides AI-driven recommendations, and triggers one-click workflows.
<br>
<br>


Features:
<br>1. The interface splits cleanly across a persistent dynamic top navigation banner

<br>2. Deep PDF parsing and visual impact breakdown (Charts and Pie Visualizations using Green for Positive Impact and Red for Negative Impact/Risk)

<br>3. Contract timeline tracking for upcoming renewals, missing obligations, and payment milestones.

<br>4. Embedded AI assistant capable of running **locally** to preserve document privacy.

<br>5.Provides real-time guidance, next steps, and risk mitigation strategies tailored to the analyzed file.

<br>6.One-Click Action Workflows:
<br>-----Instant one-click triggers to send NDA agreements, sign pending documents, issue renewal alerts, and follow up on milestones. 

<br>7.**Dynamic contract lifecycle management:** Fully editable system to add, update, or remove contracts and compliance logs on demand.
